## SPELL CHECK — the decisive rule

The 69 shows **two distinct classes**:

> "The words highlighted in **Red** are misspelled."
> `rebasing` · `webhook` · `idempotency`
>
> "**Re-examine** the spellings."
> `Soniox` · `JSONL` · `DEFINER` · `Duffing` · `WebSockets` · `Supabase`
>
> "Note: Words highlighted in yellow will not result in any deduction of marks."

Status here is **Needs Work!** and Presentation is 10/30 — so **red words DO
deduct**; yellow ones do not.

**The discriminator is capitalisation.** Every red word is entirely lowercase;
every yellow word carries a capital or is CamelCase/all-caps:

| Red (deducts) | Yellow (free) |
|---|---|
| rebasing, webhook, idempotency | Soniox, JSONL, DEFINER, Duffing, WebSockets, Supabase |

This holds on the 93 resume too — Toolchains, vLLM, FastAPI, MLOps, BFCL,
CNMAT, Audealize, SocialFX, DAFx, Pydantic, SonAura, Qwen-7B-Chat are all
capitalised, all yellow, and cost nothing ("On Track!").

So: unknown token + any uppercase letter -> treated as a proper noun, surfaced,
not penalised. Unknown token + all lowercase -> counted as a misspelling.
